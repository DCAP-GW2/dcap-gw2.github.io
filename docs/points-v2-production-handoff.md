# Production integration handoff after Stage 4C

Stage 4C verifies a local fictional DEV.2 frontend. It does not authorize production activation. Historical DEV reconciliation and live DEV Apps Script verification are already closed; repeat them only for identified drift. This handoff supersedes the older Stage 3 list of unresolved historical decisions.

1. **Fresh complete source population.** Pin a new complete source-ledger snapshot through the proposed cutover. Inventory additions, modifications and deletions since the accepted historical snapshot separately. Preserve source observation time and distinguish it from unknown modification times.
2. **Retroactive coverage.** Run approved v2 scoring over every eligible source entry through cutover, including later entries, using the unchanged registry and accepted decisions. Resolve genuinely new evidence/policy cases through the authorized owner process; do not infer a general Other Commander score from individual historical exceptions.
3. **Final totals and reconciliation.** Independently reconcile the refreshed complete population, numerical awards, exact exceptions, ineligible, pending, suppressed and identity-withheld controls. The pinned historical total is not the eventual production leaderboard. Account for all differences without inventing zero-point awards.
4. **Identity verification.** Independently verify new/changed members and preserve existing opaque public routes. Retain explicit missing-ID/withheld handling. Never derive routes from display names or publish private identity mappings.
5. **Production contract.** Approve a distinct final production contract with exact publication, completeness, freshness, revision, effective-date and current-season semantics. DEV.2 requires inactive flags and cannot be activated as production merely by changing a client switch.
6. **Versioned endpoint.** Provide a trusted versioned public endpoint with immutable revision selection and checksum binding. Validate publisher authority, transport, source completeness, response limits and every projected aggregate. Keep private decision/source fingerprints outside the feed.
7. **Active revision metadata.** Explicitly select the production-active revision and final effective date. Decide how clients observe an intentional revision transition and fail closed on inconsistent page/profile data, including when all browser storage is unavailable.
8. **Freshness and season semantics.** Define operational freshness thresholds, historical versus live labels, current-cycle selection and reward gating from approved current data. Fresh cache receipt alone cannot establish a fresh source or reward entitlement.
9. **Production client integration.** Configure the reviewed endpoint only in an exact approved release artifact. Use a separate production v2 cache namespace; test v1/DEV.1/DEV.2 isolation and coordinated invalidation. Verify navigation, all views, missing identities, current/historical cycles and rollback before activation.
10. **Version 4 transition.** Refresh deployment/trigger/property inventories, explicitly review the secondary Version 4 deployment and decide any transition. Do not automatically retire existing deployments, including Versions 4 or 9.
11. **Backup and restore.** Independently back up production sheets/ledger, identity state, Apps Script source/properties, triggers/deployments, public feed, website and release/cache configuration. Verify a restore independently before cutover.
12. **Coordinated rollback.** Rehearse rollback of data, feed revision, client and caches as one operation. Preserve v1 compatibility and define detection thresholds, operator steps and evidence needed to declare recovery.
13. **Single operator window.** Agree one supervised publisher/operator, a no-concurrent-write window, exact execution order, validation checkpoints and stop/rollback conditions.
14. **Release rehearsal.** Validate fresh complete production artifacts with independent calculations, end-to-end browser tests, privacy checks and operational monitoring. Recheck Pages publishing and all workflow/deployment paths immediately before any approved cutover.
15. **Final exact approval.** Obtain separate explicit approval for the final artifacts, deployment transition, timings and rollback plan. Only then consider merging/activation through the approved production process.

No step in this document was executed as a production action by Stage 4C. PR #16 remains open, draft and unmerged; production v1 remains the default. No Google Sheet, Apps Script, trigger, property, published DEV revision, production feed, Pages setting or live website was modified.
