📅 About the schedule

All events are fixed to their UTC weekday and start time. Local dates may differ, including crossing midnight. Clock times can shift during daylight saving changes.

🤝 Volunteer to lead

Events may change or be cancelled depending on commander availability. Want to command, taxi, block or help set up? Tell us on Discord!

⚔️ Raids and alternatives

Activities joined by “or” are options, not simultaneous confirmed events. Activities joined by “→” are intended to follow one another.
